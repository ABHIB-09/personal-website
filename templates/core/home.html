AI Due Diligence Analyst

Trust RAG++

Evidence-oriented RAG · Confidence Scoring

Trust RAG++

Establishes a continuously informed analytical environment in which information obtained through historical and real-time web interactions is incorporated into the assessment pipeline. Retrieved evidence is subjected to contextual processing, cross-source comparison, and relevance evaluation. Beyond conventional response generation, the system introduces an evidence-oriented assessment layer that evaluates the degree of support associated with individual findings. Multiple indicators are derived from source credibility, evidentiary consistency, temporal relevance, cross-source agreement, citation coverage, and claim-level verification. These indicators are consolidated into an interpretable confidence and trust assessment, enabling each analytical conclusion to be accompanied by an indication of its evidentiary strength and reliability.

RAG Evidence Scoring Cross-source Validation Trust Metrics
